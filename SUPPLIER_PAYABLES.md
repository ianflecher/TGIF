# Supplier purchasing cycle

Implemented 16 October 2026. Finance opens `/finance/supplier-payables`; suppliers open `/supplier/invoices` from their portal navigation. The existing green interface is retained.

## Operating flow

1. Issue a purchase order; the owning supplier accepts it and reports dispatch. Dispatch does not receive stock.
2. Warehouse records passed/failed quantities using Receiving. Only passed goods are available for invoice matching.
3. The supplier submits an invoice with its unique invoice number, dates and billed PO lines. An administrator can enter an invoice on the supplier's behalf with both Finance read/write and Procurement write permissions. Submitted invoices preserve their line values; rejection preserves the record and reason. Corrected invoices use a distinct revision number.
4. Finance selects the invoice to see billed quantity, PO price, accepted quantity, quantities reserved by other approved invoices and any differences. Exact price matching is required. Missing/rejected goods, excess quantities, duplicate inventory lines on a PO and differing prices block approval. Resolve the business discrepancy or reject the invoice; there is no silent tolerance override.
5. A different administrator approves an internally entered invoice. Supplier submissions can be approved by authorized Finance staff. Select an active inventory/expense debit and supplier payable liability account. Approval reserves received quantities and creates a draft journal. Submit, independently approve and post it in Ledger controls before requesting payment.
6. Finance requests a partial or full payment. Pending/approved requests reserve the remaining amount to prevent overlapping overpayments. A different Finance approver approves the request. Cancellation requires a reason and releases an unpaid reservation.
7. After money has actually been paid outside the system, enter its date, unique bank/cash voucher reference and cash/bank asset account, then record it against the approved request. This creates a separate draft payable-debit/cash-credit journal. Independently approve/post it through Ledger controls. Recording is not a bank transfer.

## Controls and accounting boundaries

Supplier ownership and active status are checked on every supplier operation. Finance and Procurement actions have server-side permissions; locked component IDs and rechecked source ownership prevent client replacement. Database transactions lock the PO before invoices and payment requests so approvals serialize against received-goods allocations and invoice balances. Unique request keys and normalized invoice/payment references protect retries and duplicates. Supplier submission events retain supplier identity separately from internal user audit logs.

Operational paid/outstanding values use recorded cash payments; journal status is displayed separately. Drafts do not affect ledger reports. Supplier journal posting checks the source's exact accounts, amount, date and approved/recorded state. Generic reversal is blocked for supplier source journals; use the credit/payment correction workflow below to preserve operational and ledger balances. Recorded payments cannot be silently cancelled or deleted.

Amounts are in the existing single-currency ledger. PO prices are matched as supplied; separate VAT/withholding, landed cost, exchange rates, invoice attachments/OCR and bank gateway execution are not included. Finance must select accounts according to its approved policy; accepting stock alone creates no new financial journal in this implementation. Failed goods do not become invoiceable. This does not qualify statutory tax handling or inventory accrual policy.

## Supplier corrections, returns and refunds

Open an approved invoice in Finance → Supplier payables. Suppliers can see their own credit and correction history and balances, but cannot approve or mutate Finance records.

- **Discounts / overbilling:** request a supplier credit note using the agreed note number, date, reason and credit amount per original invoice line. Leave units sent back at zero. A different Finance approver reviews it. Approval debits the original payable and credits the original inventory/expense account through a new journal draft; stock does not move.
- **Physical returns:** enter both the credit amount and units actually sent back. Amount must equal original unit price × returned units. Requester and approver need Inventory write permission in addition to their Finance permissions. Approval checks remaining original quantities/credits and current on-hand stock, removes units once and records original-cost stock-out transactions atomically with the credit journal. The approver confirms that the goods were actually sent. Original invoice/PO/receipt records stay intact. Previously billed quantities remain allocated, so a returned line cannot be billed again against the same original receipt.
- **Supplier cash refunds:** after a paid invoice is credited, the balance shows money due back from the supplier. Once cash has actually been received, request a refund correction against an original payment with its unique bank reference, date, amount and evidence. A different approver confirms the receipt. Pending refunds reserve the available refund due; approved refunds are bounded by that balance and the original payment. Refunds debit the original payment's cash account and credit the original payable through a draft journal. No money is transferred by the application.
- **Erroneous payment entries:** when the recorded payment never actually occurred, request a full-entry void with a unique correction voucher and reason. A different approver confirms it. The compensating cash-debit/payable-credit journal and balance adjustment preserve the original posted payment. The invoice balance reopens, allowing a correctly approved replacement payment. This is not a cancellation of a real bank transfer. Partial voids, voiding refunded entries and silent deletion are blocked.

Post each approved correction journal through Ledger controls before continuing settlement or another correction. Correction dates must belong to an open financial period at approval; period closure is blocked while approved correction journals remain draft. Posting rechecks source accounts, amount, date and original posted journals. Original sources and approved corrections cannot be independently reversed through the generic journal reversal action.

If pending payment requests exceed the balance after a proposed credit, cancel those requests explicitly before approving the credit. Reject pending credits/refund corrections with an audited reason to resolve bad requests and release refund reservations. A correction dated in a now-closed period must be rejected and resubmitted with a distinct revision/reference and an appropriate current open-period date. Original evidence remains in history.

The reconciliation panel compares the invoice's signed operational payable position with linked posted journals, expected cash with posted cash, and approved returned quantities with stock movements **per inventory item**. Draft journals produce visible differences rather than being counted as posted. Negative payable positions mean a refund is due from the supplier. There is no automatic cross-invoice credit application.

Scope limits: returns use pooled on-hand inventory and the original invoice unit price, not lot/serial selection or a replacement-delivery workflow. A line's cumulative credits cannot exceed its original value; a prior discount may prevent a later full-price return of every unit. Separate statutory tax/withholding adjustments, foreign exchange, bank execution and changes to an actual payment's cash account remain outside this implementation. Corrections to approved correction records require Finance reconciliation; they cannot be silently edited or voided again.
